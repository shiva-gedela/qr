Reimagining Event Invitations & Guest Navigation

QRouteX connects event hosts and their guests through smart vector QR invitations, direct turn-by-turn Google Maps GPS navigation, and collaborative live moments.

OUR VISION

No More Lost Guests. Ever.

Every celebration is unique, yet guests frequently struggle to locate event venues, find parking entrances, or keep track of schedule changes.

QRouteX solves this by encoding precise Google Maps GPS coordinates into dynamic QR codes that can be printed on cards or shared digitally on WhatsApp. With one scan, guests launch turn-by-turn GPS driving directions right to your venue.
